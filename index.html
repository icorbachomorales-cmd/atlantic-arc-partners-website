01 / About

A practice built at the intersection of public mandate and private capital.

Founded by a career spanning multilateral development banks, investment banking, and infrastructure private equity.

Atlantic Arc Partners was founded on the conviction that the infrastructure gap in Latin America, the Caribbean, and Southern Europe is rarely a shortage of capital — it is a shortage of structuring. Projects stall at the seam between concessional development finance and commercial investment discipline.

Our practice sits at that seam. We bring a foundation in civil engineering and transport planning, advisory experience across Multilateral Banks, and transaction execution from investment banking and private equity — a combination that lets us speak the language of both a development bank credit committee and a private investment committee.

That private equity experience runs on both sides of the table: raising capital from institutional investors for infrastructure-focused funds, and deploying that capital into direct investments — underwriting, structuring, and closing transactions from origination through execution.

We work as a lean, senior-led advisory partner: engaged directly on the mandates that matter, without the overhead of a large institution.
